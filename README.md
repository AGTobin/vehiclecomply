Privacy Policy – Vehicle Comply
Last updated: September 2026
Vehicle Comply is designed to help organisations complete and manage digital vehicle safety inspections.
Information We Collect
To provide the service, Vehicle Comply stores information that you or your organisation enters, including:
- Account email address
- Driver name
- Vehicle information
- Inspection results
- Inspection notes
- Driver signatures
- Photographs attached to inspections (if used)
This information is stored securely using Google's Firebase services to enable authorised users within your organisation to access inspection records across multiple devices.
How We Use Your Information
The information collected is used solely to:
- Authenticate users
- Associate users with their organisation
- Store inspection reports
- Generate PDF inspection reports
- Maintain an inspection history
- Enable authorised users to access reports
Vehicle Comply does not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
Analytics and Tracking
Vehicle Comply does not use advertising services or cross-app tracking.
Data Security
Inspection data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud services. Access to inspection records is restricted to authorised users within the relevant organisation.
Your Data
Your organisation controls the inspection data entered into Vehicle Comply. If you require access to or deletion of your data, please contact your organisation administrator.
Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:
