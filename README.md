Vehicle Comply-privacy statement Privacy Policy – Vehicle Comply

Vehicle Comply does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users.

All information entered into the app, including driver name, vehicle details, inspection data, and signatures, is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

The app does not use analytics, tracking, or third-party services that collect user data.

Any PDF reports generated by the app are created locally on the device and can be shared by the user using their preferred method (such as email or file sharing). The app does not retain or access these files once they are shared.

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact: http://www.vehiclecomply.co.uk

This policy may be updated in future versions of the app.
